The official starting point

The official FEAST training platform is free. EUROCONTROL’s candidate FAQ says it covers almost all FEAST I tests, with one hardware-related exception, and does not cover FEAST II. Your recruiter chooses which tests to use.

EUROCONTROL recommends trying each practice test, reviewing the result and repeating it once or twice, with roughly eight hours as a maximum overall guideline. Real instructions and items can differ; read them afresh on test day.

A practical preparation checklist

  1. Read your invitation for the schedule, equipment, language and documents to bring.
  2. Use the official platform to understand the tasks; note any instruction you misread.
  3. Follow the provider’s practice guidance instead of treating perfection on a practice score as a requirement.
  4. Prepare any additional interview or recruitment stages separately, and protect your sleep before testing.

This checklist is our suggested way of organising preparation. It does not promise improved test scores or replace your recruiter’s advice.

Make a regular practice routine

Use short arithmetic, memory or divided-attention sessions to keep your practice consistent. Review mistakes after each session, then choose a specific exercise to return to next time.

In the app, open Airways, Psychomotor, 2-back memory, Shapes & colors, Calculation grid, Cube nets and 3D objects from the catalogue. This routine practises related skills; it does not reproduce FEAST items. ICNA is a different route: the French civil-service competition has no aptitude tests and is not FEAST (see the ICNA guide, in French).
