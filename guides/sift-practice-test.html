What the SIFT covers

The Selection Instrument for Flight Training (SIFT) is the US Army's flight aptitude test. Its subtests are Simple Drawings, Hidden Figures, Army Aviation Information, Spatial Apperception, Reading Comprehension, Math Skills and Mechanical Comprehension. Army recruiting publishes the official guidance.

How to practise

Simple Drawings and Hidden Figures reward fast, accurate visual scanning; Spatial Apperception asks you to read an aircraft's attitude from a view out of the cockpit. Brain Preflight's code comparison and shapes exercises train fast visual checking, map heading and 3D objects train orientation, and the calculation grid trains maths speed. Study aviation information and mechanical comprehension separately.
