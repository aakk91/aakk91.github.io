Try two original practice questions

These Brain Preflight examples focus on checking and navigation.

1. Check a code precisely

Are these two codes exactly the same?

RX7B-4826
RX7B-4862

Show the answer and method

Different. The final two digits are swapped: 26 versus 62. Compare positions rather than reading the overall shape. Both codes contain the same characters, which makes this easy to miss.

2. Follow a permitted route

A destination is two blocks east and one block north of you. Each block has equal length. The first street east is one-way in the opposite direction. The street north is open, and the next parallel eastbound street is open. What is the shortest valid route?

Show the answer and method

One block north, then two blocks east: 3 blocks. Check whether a route is permitted before counting its length. The direct-looking eastbound start is invalid because of the one-way restriction.

A five-session daily dispatcher practice routine

  1. Same or not: begin with exact comparisons and notice which character positions you overlook.
  2. Map reading: distinguish a heading question from a shortest-route question.
  3. Street scene or 2-back memory: choose detailed recall or continuous memory updating.
  4. Psychomotor: practice maintaining one task while responding to another.
  5. Your choice: repeat the weakest task, or choose Calculation grid for arithmetic.

Review mistakes by cause: a transposition, a missed one-way arrow, a detail assigned to the wrong object or an interrupted tracking task. This gives you a specific target for the next session. Keep the same exercise for several sessions when you want to compare your progress.
