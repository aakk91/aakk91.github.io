Start with the FAA assessment instructions

The Air Traffic Skills Assessment (ATSA) is an employment aptitude assessment for FAA air traffic controller applicants. It is not the same assessment as EUROCONTROL’s FEAST. Pearson VUE’s FAA page explains booking and test-day arrangements. Follow your invitation for the requirements that apply to you.

Build a practice plan around errors

Try a short mental arithmetic, memory or spatial exercise first. Review incorrect answers without a timer. Record whether the problem was understanding an instruction, doing the calculation or keeping information in mind. Then repeat a comparable session and compare accuracy as well as speed. This is a suggested practice method, not a prediction of your ATSA result.

Practise divided attention separately

A combined exercise can expose mistakes that do not appear when you work on one task alone. Brain Preflight includes a psychomotor exercise where you track a target while answering other prompts, plus memory, reasoning and spatial exercises. Use these to practise their mechanics. A touchscreen exercise does not reproduce the equipment, scoring or content of the official ATSA.

Before test day

Use the official booking instructions to check your identification, location and arrival time. Pearson VUE recommends rest, a meal and allowing enough time to reach the test centre. Avoid treating an app score as a hiring threshold. Recruitment decisions and qualifying standards belong to the FAA.

Questions about your preparation

Can I practise ATSA-related skills for free?

Yes. Brain Preflight offers 5 free iPhone exercise sessions immediately and every day for maths, memory, spatial reasoning and divided attention. Choose any available exercise without a purchase or subscription. This is related-skills practice, not the official FAA assessment.

Is ATSA the same test as FEAST?

No. ATSA is the FAA assessment discussed in this guide; FEAST is a separate air traffic control assessment system. Start from the assessment named in your recruitment invitation, then use the relevant guide and official candidate instructions.

What should I review after an ATSA practice session?

Record whether an error came from forgetting information, misunderstanding a rule, rushing a calculation or losing track of concurrent tasks. Choose the next exercise to address that error. An app score does not predict an FAA result or hiring decision.
