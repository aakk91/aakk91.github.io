Practise with Brain Preflight

Brain Preflight is an iPhone app with short aptitude exercises: mental arithmetic, memory, spatial reasoning, attention and a psychomotor tracking task. Start with 5 free sessions immediately and get 5 more every day, choosing any exercise, without a purchase or subscription. Installed exercises work offline. Optional Premium adds unlimited sessions, mock exams and detailed diagnostics. The interface is available in English, French, German, Spanish and Dutch. The catalogue includes 32 exercises, accessible from All in each interface language. Some exercises use their original content language.

Questions about your preparation

Can I practise AFOQT-related skills for free on iPhone?

Yes. Brain Preflight includes 5 free sessions each day for arithmetic, spatial reasoning and visual checking. These short exercises can accompany a separate AFOQT knowledge study plan; they do not replace every subject in a course or study guide.

How do app drills differ from an AFOQT study guide?

App drills provide short interactive practice with feedback on cognitive tasks. A study guide or course can cover academic material and explain exam subjects in depth. Compare the subjects you need with the coverage of each resource.

Can I use Brain Preflight alongside other AFOQT resources?

Yes. Use the app’s daily allowance for a specific skill you want to revisit and keep separate time for verbal, academic and aviation study. Follow the official AFOQT candidate resources linked in our AFOQT guide.
