Brain Preflight is an iPhone app with 19 short aptitude exercises in English: mental arithmetic, memory, spatial reasoning, attention and a psychomotor tracking task. Start with 5 free sessions immediately and get 5 more every day, choosing any exercise, without a purchase or subscription. Installed exercises work offline. Optional Premium adds unlimited sessions, mock exams and detailed diagnostics. The app is also available in French, with 20 exercises, and in German, Spanish and Dutch, with 15.
