The short answer: a screening is not the same as a certificate

SPHAIR includes an aptitude-screening stage and a flight-course stage. A DLR certificate comes from its own assessment process. For Swiss EFA training, a qualifying SPHAIR certificate can be an alternative to the DLR certificate, subject to the published conditions. This is not a choice between two app scores: first establish which official document your application needs.

A useful decision order

  1. Identify the destination: SWISS, Edelweiss, military flying or another airline. A Swiss route should not be assumed valid for every airline.
  2. Check whether you meet SPHAIR’s participation conditions. If you do not, read the DLR/EFA route requirements rather than assuming that the same eligibility rules apply.
  3. If you already hold a SPHAIR certificate, read the actual recommendation and compare its date with EFA’s current rules. Ask the provider about unclear or older documents.
  4. Prepare the next stage you actually need. For SPHAIR screening, build a broad skills routine and use SPHAIR material. For DLR, use the official information supplied for your assessment.

What changes after a qualifying SPHAIR certificate?

A qualifying certificate may remove the need for the separate DLR certificate in the specified Swiss EFA route. It does not by itself replace all later assessment, interviews, airline-fit steps or medical requirements. EFA requires the commercial-pilot recommendation and a SPHAIR certificate no older than five years on the day of the Step 2 Lufthansa Group assessment. Check those conditions directly when planning your application. Keep the decision separate from commercial training offers that describe an entire career as a single test.

Frequently asked questions

Can SPHAIR replace DLR for SWISS or Edelweiss?

EFA describes an alternative with a qualifying completed SPHAIR certificate. Its commercial-pilot recommendation and validity conditions apply; the screening alone is not the certificate.

Is a SPHAIR certificate valid for every airline?

Do not assume so. Check the exact airline and training-provider requirements. The alternative described here concerns the specified Swiss EFA route for SWISS and Edelweiss.
