When Brain Preflight is a useful alternative

You might be preparing across several skill families, or want French, Spanish or Dutch practice. You can train five times a day without paying, and return for another five sessions the next day. Premium is optional. A sample session could combine a calculation grid, a cube-net exercise and a psychomotor round, then a review of your mistakes.

When the Lite app fits better

SkyTest BU/GU Lite offers its named DLR-oriented modules through an upfront app purchase. Brain Preflight gives you a free daily route into mixed aptitude practice. Stay with five free sessions a day, or choose Premium for unlimited sessions and additional features.
