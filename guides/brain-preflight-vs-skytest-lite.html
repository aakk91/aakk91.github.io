Questions about your preparation

Is there a free daily alternative to SkyTest Lite?

Brain Preflight offers 5 free daily iPhone sessions, including maths, memory, spatial, attention and mechanical comprehension practice. The exercises train related DLR skills. Compare the coverage in this guide and use any official familiarisation supplied for your selection.

Does Brain Preflight reproduce the official DLR test?

No. The app’s exercises, levels and mock-exam routines are its own practice formats. They are not official DLR items, certificates or ratings. Follow the current DLR and training-provider instructions for your application.

Can I prepare DLR technical understanding in the app?

Yes. Mechanical comprehension covers gears, levers, pulleys, springs and pressure in English, French and German. It supports technical reasoning practice, but it does not replace the full physics, English or other preparation your selection may require.
