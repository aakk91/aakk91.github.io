What the ASTB-E covers

The ASTB-E has a Math Skills Test (MST), Reading Comprehension Test (RCT), Mechanical Comprehension Test (MCT), Aviation and Nautical Information Test (ANIT), the Naval Aviation Trait Facet Inventory (NATFI) and Performance Based Measures (PBM). Navy sources describe the PBM battery with spatial orientation, dichotic listening and stick-and-throttle tracking tasks. Check the Navy's official ASTB information for the current battery and retake rules.

How to practise

Study the knowledge sections (ANIT, MCT) with dedicated material. For speed and the PBM, short daily drills help: Brain Preflight's calculation grid for MST speed, cube and 3D-object exercises for spatial work, and the psychomotor exercise for tracking while answering other prompts. The app has no listening exercise, so it does not practise dichotic listening. These are related skills, not official ASTB items.

In the app, open these exercises from the catalogue: Calculation grid, Logic sequences, Cube nets, 3D objects, Stacks, Psychomotor, Airways and Shapes & colors.
