Check your recruitment route

The RCMP’s Federal Policing recruitment page describes an Online Entrance Assessment covering workstyle, language comprehension, numerical skills, spatial skills, memory and business reasoning. It also links to a preparatory guide. Use the instructions sent by your recruiter: provincial police services and other routes can use different processes.

Cognitive practice has a specific role

For numerical practice, review the method behind each wrong answer before increasing speed. For spatial questions, work out which features remain the same after rotation. For memory exercises, note whether you lose the items themselves or their order. These are ways to structure practice, not claims that app scores predict recruitment results.

Workstyle is not a maths test

The assessment includes personality and behaviour as well as cognitive ability. Do not approach workstyle questions as a puzzle with a hidden ideal persona. Read the official instructions and answer honestly. Brain Preflight does not supply answers to personality or suitability assessments.

Using Brain Preflight alongside the official guide

The app provides general reasoning, mental arithmetic, memory and spatial exercises. You can use it for short practice sessions and review your performance. It is not the RCMP assessment, an official simulator or a complete police recruitment course. Start with the official preparatory guide before deciding what extra practice you need.

Questions about your preparation

Can I practise RCMP-related aptitude skills for free?

Yes. Brain Preflight gives 5 free sessions every day on iPhone for logic, memory, arithmetic and attention. These are general cognitive exercises. Read the RCMP candidate information linked in this guide for your actual assessment requirements.

Does Brain Preflight simulate the complete RCMP assessment?

No. General cognitive drills do not reproduce the complete assessment or replace the recruitment process. Prepare the stages specified in your invitation separately and use official RCMP guidance for assessment instructions.

Is RCMP preparation the same as Québec police preparation?

No. Recruitment processes vary by organisation and route. Use the RCMP guide for that application and the separate ENPQ guide for the Québec school’s tests. Do not assume a practice routine covers every police selection.
