What the app does not cover

Workstyle and personality questionnaires, situational judgement, language tests, physical tests and interviews need other preparation. Answer behavioural questionnaires honestly. The app practises related cognitive skills and does not reproduce any official test.

Practise with Brain Preflight

Brain Preflight is an iPhone app with 19 short aptitude exercises in English: mental arithmetic, memory, spatial reasoning, attention and a psychomotor tracking task. Start with 5 free sessions immediately and get 5 more every day, choosing any exercise, without a purchase or subscription. Installed exercises work offline. FAQ

Is there a free aptitude test practice app for Canadian police and military?

Yes. Brain Preflight is free on iPhone with 5 sessions every day on any exercise. It covers cognitive parts of the RCMP and provincial police assessments, and general practice for Canadian Armed Forces aircrew testing.

Does the app prepare the ENPQ entry tests?

No. The ENPQ tests are a situational judgement test and a behavioural questionnaire, and the ENPQ says they need no specific preparation.
