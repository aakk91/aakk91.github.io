Where the aptitude assessment fits in a SWISS application

For ab initio candidates, the European Flight Academy describes a certificate stage before the application and Lufthansa Group assessment. SWISS also publishes an airline-specific selection factsheet. Keep the certificate, the group assessment and the airline fit separate in your planning: passing one stage does not automatically complete the next. This guide concerns cognitive preparation for a trainee pilot application, rather than an experienced-pilot simulator assessment.

DLR certificate or qualifying SPHAIR certificate?

The Swiss EFA route accepts a DLR certificate or a qualifying SPHAIR certificate. For the SPHAIR alternative, the commercial-pilot recommendation and certificate age at the Lufthansa Group assessment matter. Do not assume that attending a SPHAIR screening is enough: it is the completed programme and the relevant certificate that count. Check the current wording with EFA, then use our SPHAIR-versus-DLR decision guide and SPHAIR preparation page to organise the right next step.

Two worked examples for your next session

A speed is 135 km/h. What is that in m/s?

Divide by 3.6: 135 ÷ 3.6 = 37.5 m/s. A second route is 135,000 metres ÷ 3,600 seconds. Write the units in your review log if conversion mistakes recur.

A 12-tooth gear turns at 90 rpm and drives a 36-tooth gear. How fast does the larger gear turn?

Speed is inversely proportional to tooth count: 90 × 12 ÷ 36 = 30 rpm. Since the driven gear is larger, it must turn more slowly. These are original training examples, not SWISS or DLR items.

A preparation checklist for a trainee SWISS pilot

  1. Create a baseline across the available exercise families; write down whether mistakes come from calculation, memory, rules or steering.
  2. Use three daily sessions for your weakest families and two for maintenance. Review errors before increasing speed.
  3. Keep separate time for English and German communication. Check the published EFA and airline language requirements rather than relying on a translated practice interface.
  4. Prepare real examples of teamwork, feedback, responsibility and learning for the interview stages. Rehearse clear explanations, not a supposedly ideal personality profile.
  5. Follow the official invitation for documents and logistics. Recheck the current certificate and airline conditions before paying for a test or training place.
