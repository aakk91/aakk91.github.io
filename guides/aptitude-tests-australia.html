FAQ

Is there a free aptitude test practice app in Australia?

Yes. Brain Preflight is free on iPhone with 5 sessions every day on any exercise. It has guides for the ADF Job Opportunities Assessment, ADF Officer Aviation and Airservices air traffic control.

Does the app simulate the JOA or MACTS?

No. It gives general numerical, logical, spatial and multitasking practice; the ADF does not publish MACTS subtests.
