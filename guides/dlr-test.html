What is the DLR pilot certificate?

The DLR Certificate Cockpit reports an assessment of cognitive abilities, professional competencies, personality and social competencies. It is not itself an airline admission decision. Older descriptions of a fixed BU/FQ pathway may not describe the route you are applying for today.

European Flight Academy’s FAQ describes certificate ratings from A to D and separate requirements for applying. Check the current requirements for your chosen programme rather than converting a practice percentage into a certificate rating.

DLR’s preparation schedule

DLR provides preparation access with the invitation and recommends spreading the specified sessions over three weeks where possible. Use that material to learn the actual task instructions. Our plan below helps you organise supplementary work around it.

Original practice examples with worked answers

Try these original practice examples before opening each explanation. Review the method, then try a fresh example using the same approach.

1. Mental calculation with a check

Calculate 15% of 240, then add 18.

Show answer and method

54. Ten percent of 240 is 24; five percent is half of that, 12. Add them to get 36, then add 18. Check the instruction order: adding 18 to 240 before taking 15% would solve a different problem.

2. Spatial reasoning with two turns

Imagine an arrow pointing east. Rotate it 90° anticlockwise, then 180° clockwise. Where does it point?

Show answer and method

South. East to north is the first turn. North to south is the second. Draw the intermediate orientation when reviewing an error; skipping that state can hide a reversed direction. This is a simple spatial warm-up, not a DLR test item.

3. A basic mechanics refresher

An ideal lever is balanced. A 20 N force acts 0.30 m to the left of the pivot. What downward force is needed 0.60 m to the right? Ignore the lever’s weight.

Show answer and method

10 N. Equal and opposite turning moments balance: 20 × 0.30 = F × 0.60. So F = 6 ÷ 0.60 = 10 N. Check the physical intuition: the longer arm needs less force. Both forces are perpendicular to the lever in this example.

Frequently asked questions

Can I retake the DLR certificate examination?

Yes. DLR currently states that certificate examinations can be repeated. Confirm booking conditions and the receiving airline or school’s requirements before arranging another attempt.
