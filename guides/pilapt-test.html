PILAPT Test Preparation: What to Verify and Practise

By Brain Preflight, the app developer · Checked 18 October 2026

PILAPT is a pilot aptitude assessment. If your invitation names it, confirm the current modules and preparation instructions with the organisation assessing you before choosing practice material.

A correction about the provider

An earlier version of this guide incorrectly attributed PILAPT to Symbiotics. Published research on PILAPT identifies work by People Technologies and Psytech. The historical paper by Kokorian and Valsler describes the system’s development; it does not establish the current module list at your school.

Start with the assessment you have been assigned

Ask the recruiter for the test name, permitted familiarisation materials, equipment, duration and any language or knowledge requirements. Do not assume that a list on a preparation website matches your sitting or that every school still uses the same battery.

What to practise while you prepare

  • Tracking: if included, understand the controls and how the target moves. Use any permitted practice with the actual type of equipment.
  • Arithmetic and reasoning: review errors and units before increasing speed.
  • Memory and spatial tasks: work through the instructions and practise with fresh items.
  • Divided attention: learn the rules of each concurrent task before combining them.

This is our suggested general practice checklist, not a verified list of current PILAPT modules. Mobile touch practice does not reproduce a joystick-and-pedal assessment.

Scores and comparison with other batteries

We do not rank PILAPT as easier, shorter or more sensitive to practice than COMPASS or cut-e. Those claims need evidence for the versions being compared. Ask your assessment provider about score interpretation and selection criteria; app results cannot establish your official performance.

Questions before you start

Can I retake PILAPT?

Ask the organisation running your selection about eligibility, waiting periods and how previous results are treated. This guide does not assume a universal retake rule.

Does Brain Preflight reproduce PILAPT?

No. It provides independent cognitive exercises, including touch-based multitasking, rather than a complete PILAPT simulation or an official score prediction.
