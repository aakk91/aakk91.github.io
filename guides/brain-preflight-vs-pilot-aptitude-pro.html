What makes Brain Preflight a different choice?

You can move between distinct exercise types instead of making every session about the same skill. For example, start with number sequences, move to cube nets and finish with a concurrent tracking-and-arithmetic round. That variety can suit someone who wants to organise practice around several weaknesses or a selection involving more than one skill family.

The free access is a daily training option: use five sessions today, then return for five more tomorrow. You can keep that routine without taking a Premium subscription. The app is available in 5 languages: English, French, German, Spanish and Dutch.

Why someone might choose Pilot Aptitude Pro

Pilot Aptitude Pro focuses on capacity and multitasking gameplay through an upfront app purchase. Brain Preflight gives you a free daily route into a wider exercise catalogue. Stay with five free sessions a day, or choose Premium for unlimited sessions and additional features.

Questions about your preparation

Is there a free daily alternative to Pilot Aptitude Pro?

Brain Preflight is free to download on iPhone and gives 5 sessions immediately and every day. You can choose any exercise available in your selected language. Compare the skills and access model in this guide to decide what fits your preparation.

Can I practise pilot aptitude exercises offline?

Installed Brain Preflight exercises work offline. Download and open the app before relying on it away from a connection. Purchases, rewarded ads and other online services still require internet access.

Which pilot aptitude app should I choose?

Start with the skills named in your selection invitation, then compare exercise coverage, controls, language, daily access and feedback. Choose the tool that addresses your actual practice gaps. No app can guarantee admission or replace the recruiter’s instructions.
