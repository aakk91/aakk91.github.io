Which test do you take?

  • AFOQT: Air Force officer applicants. It mixes knowledge and aptitude subtests.
  • TBAS: Air Force pilot and RPA pilot candidates, in addition to the AFOQT. Both feed the PCSM score.
  • ASTB-E: Navy, Marine Corps and Coast Guard aviation applicants.
  • SIFT: Army aviation applicants, including ROTC aviation cadets.

Your recruiter or programme confirms which test applies to you. A practice routine for one test is not a routine for all four.
