What the app does not cover

Knowledge sections (reading, mechanical, aviation, physical science), situational judgment and personality inventories, physical tests and interviews need other preparation. Official items are not public: the app practises related skills, and app scores do not convert into test scores. Each guide lists its official sources.

FAQ

Is there a free aptitude test practice app for US selections?

Yes. Brain Preflight is free on iPhone with 5 sessions every day on any exercise. It has guides and exercise lists for the AFOQT, TBAS, ASTB-E, SIFT, ATSA, NYPD exam and CritiCall.

Does Brain Preflight reproduce official US tests?

No. It practises related skills such as maths, memory, spatial reasoning, visual checking and multitasking.
