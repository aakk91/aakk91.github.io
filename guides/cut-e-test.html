What should you practise for a cut-e/Aon assessment?

Aon’s Assessment Solutions was formerly cut-e. The official candidate preparation page provides explanations, videos and practice resources. Match those resources to your invitation; a general list of Aon products is not your assigned test battery.

Our practical suggestion: make one note per assigned module with its input method, response rule, time limit and allowed aids. Keep that preparation note separate from the assessment itself and follow the rules about what you may use during the test.

Scoring and pacing

Aon says incorrect answers generally reduce performance-test scores. Read the instructions for your specific module before choosing a pacing strategy. Do not assume that rapid guessing helps, or that an unrelated app’s score uses the same scoring rules.

Original practice examples with worked answers

Try these original practice examples before opening each explanation. Review the method, then try a fresh example using the same approach.

1. Complete an equation

Fill the gap: (? × 6) − 14 = 40.

Show answer and method

9. Work backwards: add 14 to both sides to get ? × 6 = 54, then divide by 6. Check by substitution: 9 × 6 − 14 = 40. Practise recognising the required inverse operation before you reduce the time allowed.

2. Read the numbers, then assess the claim

A training centre scheduled 80 sessions in May and 100 in June. Statement: “Scheduled sessions increased by 25%.” Is the statement true, false or impossible to determine from these figures?

Show answer and method

True. The increase is 20 sessions. Divide by the starting value, 80: 20 ÷ 80 = 0.25 = 25%. Dividing by June’s 100 would answer a different question. These figures say nothing about whether the sessions actually took place.

3. Separate evidence from an assumption

Information: “All candidates in Group A completed the briefing. Some candidates in Group A booked a morning appointment.” Claim: “Every candidate who booked a morning appointment completed the briefing.”

Show answer and method

Cannot be determined. We know the briefing status of Group A, but nothing about morning appointments booked by people outside that group. Do not turn a statement about a subset into a statement about everyone.

A suggested seven-day Aon preparation plan

Use this schedule when you have a week available. It is our suggested organisation of practice, not an Aon requirement. If your deadline is closer, prioritise the official module instructions and your weakest assigned skill.

Keep a simple practice log: date, exercise, difficulty, correct answers, time and one mistake to fix. Compare sessions with the same settings. Use the log to choose what to practise next.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find official cut-e/Aon practice?

Use Aon’s candidate preparation page linked in this guide, and any preparation links supplied with your invitation. Match the practice to the modules assigned to you.

Can I pause an Aon test?

Aon says an individual test cannot be interrupted and resumed. Follow your invitation for deadlines and any breaks between modules.

What can I practise in Brain Preflight?

Try Calculation grid and Logic sequences for arithmetic and reasoning, 2-back memory for working memory, and Psychomotor for concurrent tracking, shape matching and arithmetic.

How does Brain Preflight track progress?

The app uses its own 1–9 practice levels, session history and progress views. Compare sessions on the same exercise and settings to decide what to practise next.
