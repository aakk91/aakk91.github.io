Questions before you start

Is Brain Preflight free?

Yes. Download free and start with 5 free exercise sessions straight away. You get another 5 free sessions each day, with no purchase or subscription required. Optional paid Premium unlocks unlimited practice, the app’s mock-exam mode and detailed diagnostics. The free tier may show ads.

Can I practise offline?

The installed exercises work offline. Purchases, rewarded ads, cloud features and other online services need an internet connection.

What can I practise in Brain Preflight?

Practise mental arithmetic, logic, memory, spatial reasoning and divided attention. The psychomotor exercise combines tracking, shape matching and arithmetic checks. The catalogue contains 20 exercises. The app is available in 5 languages.

How can I follow my progress?

Use the app’s 1–9 practice levels, session history and progress views to review your sessions. Premium adds detailed diagnostics to help you choose what to practise next.
