Questions before you start

Is Brain Preflight free?

You get 5 free exercise sessions per day. Paid Premium unlocks unlimited practice, the app’s mock-exam mode and detailed diagnostics. The free tier may show ads. Current subscription prices are shown in the app before purchase.

Can I practise offline?

The installed exercises work offline. Purchases, rewarded ads, cloud features and other online services need an internet connection.

What can I practise in Brain Preflight?

Practise mental arithmetic, logic, memory, spatial reasoning and divided attention. The psychomotor exercise combines tracking, shape matching and arithmetic checks. The catalogue contains 17 exercises, with availability varying by language.

How can I follow my progress?

Use the app’s 1–9 practice levels, session history and progress views to review your sessions. Premium adds detailed diagnostics to help you choose what to practise next.
